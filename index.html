Fill & Sign

Fill in and sign a PDF

Drop a PDF here (or scan a paper document with your camera), click anywhere to type, draw your signature, then download the finished file. Everything stays in your browser.

Works with normal and multi-page PDFs

About us

Fill & Sign is made by KamTech Solutions, an independent IT consultancy in Brussels. It is free to use, and it will stay free.

If it helped you and you would like to give something back, please don't pay us. Donate to a charity instead: for flood victims, for war victims, for people living in poverty. And most importantly, help someone close to you who is struggling: a relative, a friend, a neighbour.

Your files stay on your device

This tool runs entirely in your browser. Your PDFs, text and signatures are never uploaded, there is no backend that receives them, and nothing is stored or seen by us, including the site owner. Your last signature and theme choice are kept only in this browser's local storage, on your own device, so you can reuse them.
